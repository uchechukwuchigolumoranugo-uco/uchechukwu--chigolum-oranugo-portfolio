# Igbo-English Translation and Documentation
## Introduction

I am an Igbo-English translator, journalist, writer and content creator with professional experience in language translation and digital media. Through my work, I have translated and adapted content between Igbo and English while paying attention to accuracy, meaning, clarity and cultural context.

My experience in journalism has strengthened my research, writing, editing and communication skills. I am also interested in using digital technology to promote Igbo language, education, culture and heritage.
## Professional Translation Experience

I have professional experience translating between Igbo and English in my work with Voice of Nigeria (VON). My translation work has involved adapting news and informational content for Igbo-speaking audiences while maintaining the meaning, accuracy and clarity of the original material.

This experience has strengthened my ability to understand context, select appropriate language, translate ideas naturally, and communicate information effectively to different audiences.
